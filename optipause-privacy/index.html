1. Information Collection and Use

OptiPause is designed to respect your privacy. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It operates entirely offline. All your settings, timer preferences, and interval configurations are saved locally on your device and are never transmitted to any external servers.

2. App Permissions

To provide its core functionality (the 20-20-20 rule eye-care reminders), OptiPause requires certain device permissions. These are used strictly for local operations:

  • Notifications: Required to send you alerts when it is time to take a break or resume work.
  • Alarms & Reminders (Exact Alarm): Required to ensure timers and reminders fire precisely at the requested intervals, even if the device is in a low-power state.
  • Full Screen Intents / Display Over Other Apps: Used to wake up the screen and display the break reminder seamlessly, ensuring you do not miss your eye-rest session.
  • Vibrate & Wake Lock: Used to alert you via device vibration and temporarily wake the CPU to process the timer.

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party services, trackers, or analytics tools that may collect information used to identify you.

4. Data Security

I value your trust in providing us your personal information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. Since OptiPause does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no risk of data breaches or leaks from our end. Your local settings are secured by the Android operating system's standard data sandboxing.

5. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected, the app complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
